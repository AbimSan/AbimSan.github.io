RESEARCH PROFILE

Connecting Research with Security Practice

I am a cybersecurity researcher and technology risk professional with research interests spanning network security, software-defined networking, intelligent threat detection, cloud infrastructure and emerging digital technologies.

My doctoral research investigated the design and analysis of anomaly detection and mitigation schemes for Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks in Software-Defined Networks (SDN). This involved developing experimental environments, generating attack traffic, analysing network behaviour and evaluating machine learning approaches for detecting and mitigating security threats.

Beyond technical security, my research interests explore how organisations can adopt emerging technologies while maintaining appropriate levels of security, privacy, governance and resilience.

DOCTORAL RESEARCH

Design and Analysis of Anomaly Detection and Mitigation Schemes

PhD Thesis | University of Bradford | 2019

Design and Analysis of Anomaly Detection and Mitigation Schemes for Distributed Denial of Service Attacks in Software Defined Network.

My doctoral research investigated security vulnerabilities in Software-Defined Networks and developed approaches for detecting and mitigating Distributed Denial of Service attacks using machine learning techniques.

The work involved experimental testbed development, attack traffic generation, data preparation, performance evaluation and analysis of detection and mitigation approaches.

The research contributed to subsequent work on network security, machine learning based attack detection and the performance analysis of software-defined network environments.
